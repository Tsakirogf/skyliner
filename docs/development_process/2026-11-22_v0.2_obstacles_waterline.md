# 2026-11-22: skyliner v0.2, obstacles masked (no rejections) and the waterline

## Request

Photos with deck machinery, clouds or any other obstacle must not be rejected. Skyliner annotates the
obstacles, tells them apart and masks them out. When there is no open horizon, it marks the waterline.

## Changes

1. **No rejections.** v0.1 rejected 7/116 held-out photos when the horizon fit was weak. Now:
   - **Horizon evidence masks obstacles.** Sea-line candidates and refinement points within ±4–6 scene
     rows of an obstacle are dropped; v0.1's bad fits came from machinery and railing edges.
   - **`horizon_quality`:**
     - `good`: the v0.1 checks pass.
     - `low`: the best line is kept and every sample's σ gets the line uncertainty — an offset term of
       at least 3 % of the frame height, plus a 3° roll term that grows with distance from the
       evidence centre.
     - `none`: no sea in view at all. Pixel annotation only (L1, obstacles, overlay), no observation,
       because angles need an attitude.
   - **Fallbacks, in order:**
     1. sea-line evidence with obstacles masked;
     2. unmasked evidence (`low`);
     3. a level line through ≥ 4 sea points (`low`);
     4. `none`.
   - `--strict-horizon` still rejects frames without an open-sea horizon, for strict §4.2 use.
   - **Near-shore waterline:** with no open sea and land rising > 4° above the line, the frame is
     `low`. The waterline of near land sits below, and can be tilted from, the true horizon.
   - **Low-quality frames:** observation written as `observation.low_quality.json` unless
     `--emit-low-quality`.
   - **Minimum land height:** land must stand ≥ 2 px above the sea line; thinner slivers are
     `unknown`.
2. **Obstacles.**
   - **SceneNet retrained with synthetic clouds** on summits, labelled as obstacles. The dataset has no
     clouds; it is clear summer sky.
     - First attempt: grey, textured, translucent clouds. The model then flagged hazy land and the
       whitish cliff strata of IMG_5031 as obstacles.
     - Second attempt: bright, smooth, mostly opaque neutral-white clouds at 15 % probability.
   - **IMG_5031 pseudo-labels.** The hand label traces only columns 0–94. Columns 95–511 now get the v0.1
     held-out silhouette where it traced land (195 columns), so the cliff is taught as land. These
     columns are excluded from scoring.
   - **Instances** (`skyliner/obstacles.py`): connected components of the obstacle mask, grouped by a
     5-scene-pixel dilation. Pieces whose box touches a ship-structure box inherit that type (machinery
     seen through grating).
   - **Types** (heuristic, from geometry and colour): `ship_structure, vessel_object, cloud, glare,
     wake_spray, other`.
   - **Output:** `obstacle_mask.png` (photo resolution), `obstacles.json`, `occl_type` per column, and
     `reason: "obstacle: <type>"`.
3. **Waterline (L3).**
   - **Measured** in every column where land meets the sea: EdgeNet channel 3 near the SceneNet land→sea
     boundary, with the scene boundary at larger σ as fallback.
   - **Constraints:** never above the sea line, and runs shorter than 3 columns are dropped.
   - **Output:** a `waterline` layer in the observation (§8.1) plus `waterline` / `waterline_sigma`
     in `label.json`; drawn yellow in the overlay.
   - **Without an open horizon,** L0 is the line through the waterline (`horizon_source: "waterline"`),
     as before.
   - **Check:** there is no waterline ground truth. Under distant land the hand-label horizon is the
     land/sea line, and the scorer compares against it there.

- **Nothing is rejected.** All 116 photos have an attitude: 102 `good`, 14 `low`.
  - Low: IMG_4954–4957 and IMG_4973–4979 (near islands, waterline only); IMG_4991 and IMG_4993
    (deck machinery over the sea line); IMG_5031 (near cliff).
  - Low-quality frames get the full annotation. Their observation is written as
    `observation.low_quality.json`, unless `--emit-low-quality`: Horizon weighs σ only inside a bin
    today, and some low lines are far off (IMG_5031: 11.6° roll; IMG_4991: horizon 263 px off).
- **Good-quality horizons:** silhouette p90 0.046°; worst roll error 0.34° (IMG_4953).
- **`horizon_source`:** open sea 36, waterline 80.
- **SceneNet comparison**, same post-processing:

  | | v1 (no clouds) | v2 (synthetic clouds) |
  |---|---|---|
  | land recall | 0.8996 | 0.917 |
  | false land | 0.043 | 0.039 |
  | p90, good horizons | 0.051° | 0.046° |

  v2 is shipped. Its first held-out run had false land 0.055 and IMG_4976 (near island) passing
  as `good` with a 2.9° roll error. Two rules followed:
  - land < 2 px above the sea line → `unknown`;
  - waterline frames with land rising > 4° → `low` (near-shore waterline).

  Both thresholds were chosen on these out-of-fold predictions, so the figures are optimistic.
- **Clouds:** the dataset has none, so cloud handling is tested only on synthetic clouds pasted with
  unseen seeds.
  - Bright cumulus on summits: detected and skipped (IMG_5011, IMG_4963), though the masks are partial.
  - A thin, smoke-like cloud (IMG_4925): missed, and the silhouette runs over its top.
  - Real cloud photos need labelling.
- **Obstacle false positives remain** on bright, textured land: the white town on IMG_5031's rim is
  typed `cloud`, and some cliff strata are flagged. They cost recall (those columns become
  `unknown`), not correctness.
- **Waterline vs hand line under distant land:** median 0.70 px (2048 grid), 91 % of checkable
  columns.
