# 2026-11-20: skyliner v0.1, first ML model

## Goal

Build the skyline annotator specified in `SKYLINER_REQUIREMENTS.md` with ML, trained on the 116
hand-labelled ferry photos in `example_dataset/`. It produces the contract v2 observation plus every
level the hand labels have (L0, L1, inner ridges, occluders), with σ, `lower_bound` and per-column
reasons in addition.

## What the data looked like (findings)

- Labels are on a 2048-wide grid (512 columns, step 4); photos are 1600×1200. `meta.json` carries
  `focal_px`, which varies per photo (1382–5576 px: zoom/crop). EXIF is stripped, so `meta.json` is
  the focal source.
- `label.horizon` is `None` where the sea line is hidden (498/512 columns in IMG_4991). The meta line
  `horizon_a/b` is the reference: it matches the label horizon to a median of 0.04 px, and where they
  differ by more, the label follows a near waterline (IMG_4953, IMG_4931).
- My §4.2 conversion reproduces the hand `observation.json` land elevations to a median of 0.0005°
  (`tests/test_skyliner.py`).
- **52 of 116 photos have no open-sea column at all**: land spans the frame, and the hand labels use
  the land/sea line as the horizon. Only 43 photos have ≥ 12 open-sea columns.
- `occl` is all zeros; occluders were labelled as "no land", so the hand observations call them `sea`:
  - IMG_4944: ship's pillar, 231 `sea` samples.
  - IMG_4992: the outer line traces around the deck dish cover.
  - IMG_5031: the cliff silhouette is labelled in columns 0–94 only; the rest is `sea`.

  All three break the §5 rule "unknown is never sea".
- Thin vertical spikes in `outer` (masts or ships: IMG_4959, 4970, 5011, 4962) are excluded
  automatically (`labels.spike_cols`).
- `IMG_4962/observation.json` is truncated on disk at 4096 bytes. Skyliner regenerates it.

## Label corrections (`skyliner/label_fixes.json`)

These are used for training and scoring only; the original files are untouched.
- Occluder polygons: 4943, 4944, 4951 (pillars and poles); 4989 (railing, dish); 4991, 4992, 4993 (deck
  machinery); 4953 (railing). They are taught as class `occluder`, and their columns are excluded from
  silhouette scoring.
- `ignore_cols`: IMG_5031 columns 95–511 (silhouette not labelled).

## Method

Two CNNs, trained with PyTorch on CPU, shipped as ONNX (`models/`):

| net | input | output | params | train |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SceneNet (U-Net) | 512×384 | sky / land / sea / occluder | ~0.5 M | 1100 steps × 8 crops 384×256 |
| EdgeNet (FCN, full-res path + dilated half-res path) | full res, skyline band only | heatmaps: L0 open sea, L1 silhouette, L2 inner ridge, L0 under distant land | ~0.07 M | 1300 steps × 12 crops 128² |

- **Augmentation.**
  - Rotation ±4° (roll), zoom, flip.
  - Brightness, contrast, saturation, mild haze veil, blur, noise.
  - Copy-paste of the labelled occluders into other photos, plus synthetic poles and railings, to
    teach the occluder class from very few examples.
- **Targets.** EdgeNet targets are per-column Gaussians (σ = 1 px) centred on the label row, so the peak
  gives sub-pixel position.
- **Losses.** Cross-entropy with ignore for SceneNet. Weighted BCE for EdgeNet: L2 negatives count
  less, because the inner ridges are only partly labelled.
- **Post-processing** (`skyliner/infer.py`):
  - **Horizon.** A coarse line through all sky→sea and land→sea transitions, then refined.
  - **Open-sea columns.** Decided by the EdgeNet L0 peak (SceneNet always draws a thin land sliver
    at the line, as the labels do).
  - **Line fit.** A robust IRLS line over open-sea points plus land→sea points under distant land.
  - **L1.** Topmost-wins: the topmost strong EdgeNet peak near the SceneNet land top.
  - **States** per §5. Occluders count only when they touch the L1 band (land columns) or the sea-line
    band (sea columns).
  - **Lower bound.** Set when faint land (P > 0.2) sits ≥ 3 scene rows above the traced top; σ is
    inflated.
  - **σ.** Heatmap spread, mapped through an out-of-fold quantile calibration.

## Iterations during this session

1. All photos were rejected at first: SceneNet draws land down to the line, so no column went
   straight from sky to sea. Fixed by detecting open sea with the EdgeNet L0 channel.
2. With strict §4.2 rejection, 52/116 photos have no open sea. The labels use the waterline there, so
   the waterline became the default fallback (`horizon_source: "waterline"`, larger σ).
   `--strict-horizon` restores rejection.
3. Waterline photos had 3–9 px horizon error from the coarse SceneNet boundary. Added EdgeNet channel 3
   (land→sea line under distant land).
4. The occluder rule was too strict: sun flare and sea glare killed land columns. It is now band-limited.
5. Fold-0 photos with open sea on one side only had roll errors around 0.3°. Adding the channel-3
   points to the line fit gave fold-0 roll median 0.027° → 0.014° and height p90 0.075° → 0.053°.
6. First full out-of-fold run (116 photos):
   - Near-island photos 4976–4978 had the horizon 60–88 px off (roll up to 6.8°): confident-wrong.
     Fixed with a bottom-up sea region boundary and a reject guard (≥ 24 refined points over ≥ 25 %
     of the width).
   - False land was 8.4 %, mostly faint distant land the labeller called sea. Fixed with the
     faint-land rule.
   - σ coverage was off because σ had been calibrated on the L1 row error only. It is now calibrated
     on the height error, horizon included.
7. Widening the line search to recover IMG_4962 (the coarse line sat 15 px low) let wrong peaks in:
   IMG_4997 got a 19° roll, because the sea seen between the railing posts set the line. Fixes:
   - narrow-then-wide refinement;
   - agreement check against the coarse line;
   - the coarse boundary uses the topmost long sea run (≥ 8 scene rows) instead of the bottom one;
   - reject when the fit residual is > 5 px. Bad fits behind machinery had 8–11 px; good fits ≤ 3 px.

   A first cut at 3 px with a 1° coarse-line check made the final model reject 11 photos whose fits
   were correct (IMG_4955 had a 3.06 px residual with the right line), so both thresholds were relaxed.
8. Final run of the shipped model (trained on all 116) on Fotis's machine, writing to
   `annotated_dataset/`:
   - 113 annotated, 3 rejected (IMG_4991, IMG_4992, IMG_5031); 0.3–0.8 s per frame on 2 CPU cores.
   - These photos are in-sample for that model, so this is a delivery check, not a quality measure.
   - IMG_4962 (truncated hand observation) was regenerated: line b = 656.77 against the hand
     657.30.

## Results (out-of-fold, 4 contiguous folds, 116 photos)

All numbers are out-of-fold: each photo is annotated by models that never saw it. The 4 folds are
contiguous ID blocks, which is stricter than leave-one-photo-out because neighbouring frames are
near-duplicates. The final shipped model is trained on all 116 photos.

| metric | value | requirement | met |
|---|---|---|---|
| horizon_err_px2048_median | 0.6150 | <= 3 px | yes |
| roll_err_deg_median | 0.0266 | <= 0.05 deg | yes |
| height_err_deg_median | 0.0119 | <= 0.03 deg | yes |
| height_err_deg_p90 | 0.0516 | <= 0.06 deg | yes |
| land_recall | 0.9005 | >= 0.90 | yes |
| false_land | 0.0436 | <= 0.05 | yes |
| wrong_layer_frac | 0.0100 | <= 0.02 | yes |
| sigma_cov_1 | 0.7169 | ~0.68 | yes |
| sigma_cov_2 | 0.9208 | ~0.95 | yes |
| photos / rejected | 116 / 7 | as few as possible | |

- **Rejected (7),** each with a reason in `rejected.json`:
  - IMG_4974–4978: near island filling the frame; the near-island run sits in one fold, so its model
    never saw such a view.
  - IMG_4991 and IMG_4993: deck machinery hides the sea line.

  The horizon guard (≥ 24 refined points over ≥ 25 % of the width, agreement with the coarse line
  within 2°, fit residual ≤ 5 px) rejects rather than emit a possibly confident-wrong skyline.
- **Worst accepted horizon:** roll error 0.35° (IMG_4955). Every catastrophic case seen during
  development (roll 3–19°) is now rejected.
- **`horizon_source`:** open sea in 36 photos, waterline fallback in 73.
- **Tuning caveat:** the faint-land thresholds and the horizon guards were chosen while looking at these
  same out-of-fold predictions, so the figures are somewhat optimistic. A new labelled set is the honest
  test. Before the faint-land rule: false land 0.084, recall 0.929.
- **σ:** calibrated per horizon source on |height-above-line error| (`models/calibration.json`).
  Coverage is 72 % at 1σ and 92 % at 2σ: the tails are heavier than Gaussian.
- **Not measured here:** the end-to-end target (within 1 km ≥ hand labels, confident-wrong ≤ hand labels)
  needs `scripts/17_locate.py` in the Horizon repo, run on `annotated_dataset/`.

Per-photo rows: `reports/cv_v0.1/per_photo.csv`.

## Known gaps / next steps

- End-to-end localization (`scripts/17_locate.py`) has to run in the Horizon repo on
  `annotated_dataset/*/observation.json`. It was not run here.
- `.hzo` writer and MQTT: they need `horizon_contract` (geolocator repo). The JSON is
  `horizon.observation/2+json` plus the §8 fields.
- L3 waterline and `visibility_km`: no ground truth. The occluder class comes from 8 photos plus
  synthetic data.
- `lower_bound` is a heuristic on SceneNet probabilities. There is no haze ground truth (§10).
- All training data is one trip, one camera. Other cameras need a focal calibration (`sensors.py`) and
  more labelled photos.
